# DESIGN.md - UV & Melanin Educational Simulator

## Typography
- Primary Font Family: `IBM Plex Sans`, `IBM Plex Sans Thai`, sans-serif
- Monospace / Numbers: `IBM Plex Mono`, monospace

## Color Palette
- Background: Deep Dark Slate (`#090d16` / OKLCH L 0.12)
- Panels & Cards: Glassmorphic Dark (`#111827` / OKLCH L 0.18)
- Primary Accent: Indigo & Purple (`#6366f1` / `#a855f7`)
- Educational UV Accents:
  - UVA: Deep Violet / Indigo (`#a855f7`)
  - UVB: Vibrant Rose / Coral (`#f43f5e`)
  - Sunscreen Barrier: Sky Blue (`#38bdf8`)
  - Safety Low Risk: Emerald (`#10b981`)
  - Moderate: Amber / Yellow (`#f59e0b`)

## Layout & Spacing System
- Clean, open spacing rhythm (24px - 32px gap between major blocks, 16px - 20px padding in cards)
- 65-75ch maximum line length for readable educational prose
- High contrast readability (≥ 4.5:1 ratio) for Thai & English typography
