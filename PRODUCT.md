# PRODUCT.md - UV & Melanin Educational Simulator

## Overview
A web-based interactive educational simulator for university students in cosmetics science, aesthetics, and dermatology. Enables students to simulate UV radiation (UVA/UVB), base melanin density (Fitzpatrick scale), and sun protection behaviors while comparing before/after trends.

## Core Features
- UV Index & Exposure Duration Controls
- UVA vs UVB Spectrum Ratio Adjustments
- Fitzpatrick Skin Type & Natural Melanin Absorption Model
- Protective Behavior Toggles (Sunscreen SPF/PA, Apparel, Peak Hour Avoidance)
- 2D Interactive SVG Skin Layer Visualization (Epidermis, Dermis, Subcutaneous)
- Comparative Analytics Panel (Before vs After Protection)
- Educational Knowledge Hub & Academic Citations

## Target Audience
University students and instructors in Cosmetic Science, Aesthetics, Dermatology, and Health Sciences.
